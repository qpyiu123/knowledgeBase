# 阿里云CTF2025复现-风清

## 一句话总结

阿里云CTF2025复现风清:runes(MIPS+chroot+SCM_RIGHTS+AF_UNIX+send_fd/recv_fd+8次chdir逃逸+open flag)+stack use-after-scope(朴素寄存器分配+时间侧信道+exit_code -11 vs -31)+mmap BadVAddr覆盖+seccomp dump+AFL++4.08c

## 攻击链

1. runes: mmap+syscall(MIPS)+chroot+SCM_RIGHTS+send_fd+recv_fd+8次chdir(..)+open flag|stack use-after-scope: 朴素寄存器分配flush_cache后ra寄存器+time侧信道通过exit_code|chroot逃逸: mkdir foo; chroot foo; cd .. 经典套路|send_fd/recv_fd: AF_UNIX socket+SCM_RIGHTS+CMSG_DATA传fd|fchdir+open: 用收到的cwd_fd 8次..回到根|fuzz: AFL++4.08c + QEMU persistent ASAN_OPTIONS detect_leaks=0|BadVAddr覆盖: set_mem_word覆盖text_seg指令+free_inst触发UAF|mem_reference: TEXT_BOT 0x400000 / DATA_BOT 0x10000000 / STACK_TOP 0x80000000

## 经验教训

1) MIPS手搓syscall:rax=syscall号,rdi/rsi/rdx=参数;2) chroot逃逸:mkdir foo; chroot foo; cd ..;3) AF_UNIX+SCM_RIGHTS传fd:CMSG_DATA(cmsg) memcpy &fd;4) 时间侧信道:exit_code -11(SIGSEGV)/-31(SIGSYS)区分位;5) seccomp-tools dump -p `pidof binary`;6) BadVAddr覆盖:__readfsqword+xmmword+set_mem_word;7) AFL++ QEMU persistent:ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0
