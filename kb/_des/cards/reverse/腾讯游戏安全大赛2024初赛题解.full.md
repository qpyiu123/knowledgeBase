## 攻击链

1. hack.dll:DLL_PROCESS_ATTACH时AllocConsole+fopen CONOUT$ stdout,hook WriteProcessMemory
2. HookCode = mov rax,addr + jmp rax (12字节)
3. OriginFunc=WriteProcessMemory
4. VirtualProtect改PAGE_EXECUTE_READWRITE
5. memcpy备份原12字节
6. HackWriteProcessMemory:printf+GetModuleFileNameEx+调原+rehook
7. 内核驱动:L"\??\xia0ji2333"符号链接+IoCreateDevice
8. WPOFFx64:KeRaiseIrqlToDpcLevel + CR0 &= 0xfffffffffffeffff
9. inline hook:MmCopyMemory函数(内核)
10. LDR_DATA_TABLE_ENTRY遍历PsLoadedModuleList
11. IAT hook + inline hook双层

## 关键 payload

```
VirtualProtect+inline hook 12字节
```

## 经验教训

Windows hook链:DLL_PROCESS_ATTACH+AllocConsole+VirtualProtect+12字节inline hook是标准模式;内核WPOFFx64(CR0第16位WP清零)+inline hook是驱动hook基础;frida bypass需要修改re.frida_server字符串。
