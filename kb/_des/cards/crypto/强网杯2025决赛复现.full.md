## 攻击链

1. 首次收发0xC0字节(24个8字节) → 每3项求dx,dy → solve_for_k用gcd+模逆求k(候选) → 验证b=(seq[1]-k*seq[0])%2^64 → setKey得KEYA[4]/KEYB[4] → mix(X[i%4])+KEYB+KEYA*X流密码生成 → shellcode(每3字节求和%5)爆破jmp$+2花指令 → EB00 00 B9 +9B+0C字节 → openat /flag + sendfile

## 关键 payload

```
solve_for_k(dx,dy) + setKey(0xC0首包) + getKeyStream(mix+LCG) + shellcode%5爆破
```
